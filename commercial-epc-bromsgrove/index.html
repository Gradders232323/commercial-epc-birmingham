Your Bromsgrove property brief

Make the premises
clear from the start.

Existing plans may differ from the current layout. Flag subdivided rooms, removed partitions or changes in use when preparing the brief.

The full address, approximate floor area and assessment purpose are the starting point. Add current floor plans, a previous certificate or details of alterations if you have them. If information is missing, identify the gap rather than guessing.

A quotation should relate to the property being assessed. It should make clear which premises and survey work are included, how access will be arranged and what information is needed before the visit.

Before the survey

Useful information. Fewer uncertainties.

01

Define the boundary

Identify the unit, floors and supporting spaces included. Explain shared areas or equipment and name any separate occupiers.

02

Share the records

Plans, service details and evidence of completed improvements can help the assessor understand the building. Confirm whether drawings are current.

03

Arrange full access

Nominate a site contact and flag locked rooms, operational restrictions, inductions or equipment areas that need permission to enter.

04

Explain the timescale

State any sale, letting or property-management deadline. Agree availability and reporting arrangements before relying on a particular date.

Questions before you enquire

Scope first.
Then the next step.

What should I send about my Bromsgrove premises?

Existing plans may differ from the current layout. Flag subdivided rooms, removed partitions or changes in use when preparing the brief. Include the full postcode, approximate size and purpose of the assessment so the enquiry can be reviewed.

Can I start without a complete building file?

Yes. Share the records you have and explain what is unavailable. Additional details may be requested to confirm the scope and quotation.

What if the property has several uses or tenants?

Describe each part and identify the premises within your instruction. A marked plan and contacts for shared services can help clarify the proposed assessment.

How do I request a property quotation?

Use the quote page, choose Commercial EPC and include the address of your Bromsgrove property. Explain any access constraints or approaching transaction dates.
