01 · Scope and preparation

Understand what a management survey covers

Its purpose is to locate, as far as reasonably practicable, suspect asbestos-containing materials that could be disturbed during normal occupation, routine maintenance or installation of equipment. The planned access, sampling approach and limitations should be discussed before the survey.

02 · Scope and preparation

Keep intrusive works separate

A management survey is not a substitute for a refurbishment or demolition survey before intrusive work. If an alteration or demolition is planned, describe it before accepting a management-survey quotation. This page concerns management of existing commercial premises, not a new-build service.

03 · Scope and preparation

Use the survey within a management process

The survey information supports the asbestos register and management plan; a survey alone does not discharge every duty. Provide earlier reports, plans, areas of restricted access and the site contact. Known suspect materials should not be disturbed to prepare for the visit.

Before you enquire

Asbestos Management Surveys: useful answers

Does a management survey prove there is no asbestos?

No. Access and survey limitations matter, and some materials may be presumed to contain asbestos. Read the report and limitations with the register.

Will sampling always be included?

The sampling approach must be agreed for the property. Access, occupancy and material condition can affect the work.

Can it be used before refurbishment?

It should not be assumed sufficient. The planned work may require a different, more intrusive survey scope.
