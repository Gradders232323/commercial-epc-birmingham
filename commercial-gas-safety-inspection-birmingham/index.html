Tell us the property, its current use and the purpose of your enquiry. The appropriate scope, provider and arrangements are confirmed before work is agreed.

01 · Scope and preparation

Identify the installation and intended evidence

Send the address, appliance types, available model details and previous records. Explain whether the premises include commercial kitchens, plant rooms or shared systems. Different appliances and gas types can require different engineer categories and inspection scopes.

02 · Scope and preparation

Confirm competence for the work

Gas work within the applicable registration requirements must be undertaken by a Gas Safe registered engineer qualified for the specific work. Registration alone should not be taken to cover every commercial appliance. The suitable provider and availability must be confirmed before the instruction.

03 · Scope and preparation

Keep inspection, servicing and repairs clear

An inspection does not automatically include servicing, repair work or assessment of every connected system. Applicable workplace duties and any landlord responsibilities should be considered for the circumstances. Do not assume a domestic landlord timetable or a generic certificate covers every commercial installation.

Before you enquire

Commercial Gas Safety Inspections: useful answers

Is there one commercial gas certificate for every site?

No single description establishes the scope. Identify the appliances, premises and evidence required so the correct work can be agreed.

Does the inspection include servicing?

Only if specified. Inspection, servicing and remedial work should be clear in the quotation.

What if the engineer finds a safety concern?

The engineer must follow the applicable safety procedures for the circumstances. Known concerns should be reported when arranging the work; urgent situations need the appropriate emergency response.
