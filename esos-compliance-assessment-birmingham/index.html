Existing commercial premises

When this service can help

  • Organisations reviewing ESOS qualification
  • Groups coordinating evidence from existing commercial sites
  • Energy managers planning assessment and reporting work

01 · Scope and preparation

Establish whether the organisation qualifies

Qualification is assessed against the scheme rules and relevant phase. A building’s size or EPC rating alone does not answer the question. Supply group and organisational information so the relevant participation and assessment requirements can be reviewed.

02 · Scope and preparation

Agree the energy and evidence scope

ESOS can cover buildings, transport and industrial processes. Even if your initial enquiry concerns an existing building, describe the wider operations and any assessment evidence already available. A site-only audit should not be represented as complete organisation-wide compliance.

03 · Scope and preparation

Confirm responsibilities and deliverables

The quotation should identify the assessment work, any lead-assessor involvement, evidence preparation and reporting support included. Current phase requirements, action planning and notification responsibilities need checking for the organisation. The initial enquiry does not confirm qualification, assessor availability or a compliant outcome.

Before you enquire

ESOS Compliance Assessments: useful answers

Is every commercial landlord in ESOS?

No. The scheme applies to qualifying organisations under its own rules. An individual property does not automatically create a requirement.

Can existing energy audits help?

Potentially. Their coverage, quality and dates need review against the relevant scheme requirements.

Will a building inspection complete the whole process?

Not by itself. Organisational scope, evidence, assessment and reporting requirements must be considered together.
