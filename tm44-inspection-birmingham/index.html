01 · Scope and preparation

Review capacity and technical control

The inspection requirement concerns systems with an effective rated output above 12kW. Several individual units under common technical control may count together. The relevant cooling output should come from equipment records rather than an estimate based on building size.

02 · Scope and preparation

Know what the inspection is for

A TM44 inspection considers energy efficiency, controls, sizing and operation. It is separate from refrigerant leak checks, routine servicing and a commercial EPC. Describe specialist or process-related equipment separately so its purpose can be considered in the scoping discussion.

03 · Scope and preparation

Prepare for an accredited assessment

Qualifying systems require inspection at intervals no more than five years apart. A valid statutory report must be produced and lodged by an accredited air-conditioning energy assessor. Provide the equipment schedule, previous report, operating hours and access arrangements before availability and price are agreed.

Before you enquire

TM44 Air Conditioning Inspections: useful answers

Do several small units count together?

They can where they are under common technical control. Send the complete equipment schedule for review.

Does an F-Gas check replace TM44?

No. Refrigerant checks and an air-conditioning energy inspection have different purposes.

Will repairs or control adjustments be included?

Not automatically. Any maintenance or remedial work needs a separate agreed scope.
