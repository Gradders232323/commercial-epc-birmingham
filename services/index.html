Before you arrange a visit

Clear scope. Useful answers.

Can I enquire about several services?

Yes. Identify the requirements in the Birmingham quote form. Different providers, visits or reporting scopes may be needed, and the arrangements must be confirmed for each instruction.

Does a service inspection include repairs?

Not automatically. Assessment, testing, servicing and remedial works should be separately identified in the agreed quotation.

Do you cover existing buildings outside the city centre?

Enquiries can include Birmingham and surrounding West Midlands commercial areas. Provide the full postcode so suitability, travel and access can be reviewed.

Do you offer new-build services here?

This page covers existing commercial buildings. It does not promote new-build assessment or oil-storage inspection services.
