Commercial Water Hygiene Assessments Birmingham

A water-hygiene enquiry for existing Birmingham or the West Midlands premises can bring together the system layout, operating records and concerns about water quality or use. The assessment should address a defined system and purpose rather than promise a universal water-safety certificate.

Existing commercial premises

When this service can help

  • Facilities teams reviewing tanks, outlets and records
  • Managers reopening underused parts of an existing building
  • Owners checking water-hygiene arrangements after a change of occupation

01 · Scope and preparation

Define the system being reviewed

Identify the water supply, storage, hot-water arrangements and outlets included. State whether the enquiry concerns drinking water, domestic hot and cold water or a specialist system. Different systems require different expertise and assessment methods.

02 · Scope and preparation

Review records as well as visible condition

Provide previous risk assessments, schematic information, maintenance records and available monitoring results. Describe periods of low use, changes in occupation and any reported concern. The inspection scope should make clear what can be checked and what needs further investigation.

03 · Scope and preparation

Use targeted testing where it is justified

Sampling is not automatically required for every system or every enquiry. Any testing should have a defined purpose, method and interpretation. A broader hygiene review should not be represented as a replacement for a suitable Legionella risk assessment where one is needed.

People, occupation and the actual premises

For Birmingham mixed-use buildings and managed workplaces, explain the commercial boundaries, common areas and other occupiers. Earlier reports and management records can help define gaps without assuming the whole building needs the same work.

Commercial Water Hygiene Assessments: useful answers

Is this the same as a Legionella risk assessment?

No. The scope can overlap, but a general condition-and-records review should not be treated as a Legionella risk assessment unless that work is expressly included.

Will every outlet be sampled?

Not automatically. The purpose and extent of any sampling should be agreed based on the system and concerns.

Can a vacant building be reviewed?

Yes, explain the period of low use and planned reopening. The scope and any precautions need to reflect the actual circumstances.
