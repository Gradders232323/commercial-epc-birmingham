01 · Scope and preparation

Check whether the building is in scope

DEC duties depend on the building meeting the relevant public-authority occupation, public-access and floor-area conditions. A privately occupied shop does not automatically need a DEC because customers enter it. Explain the organisation occupying the premises and the accommodation included.

02 · Scope and preparation

Prepare the operational evidence

Supply the full address, floor area, current building use, available meter and consumption records and any existing certificate. Note shared supplies, separate occupiers and significant changes in use. The assessor will need to establish which energy use and area belong to the instruction.

03 · Scope and preparation

Keep the certificate and advice connected

The DEC and its associated advisory report are distinct documents with related purposes. Tell us whether you need both. The report can inform an improvement programme, but it is not a detailed design or a guarantee of savings. Any additional audit, EPC or technical investigation should be separately agreed.

Birmingham property context

Offices, public buildings and mixed occupation

For city-centre offices and separately occupied floors, identify the exact accommodation. Public-building reports need the organisation and operational evidence; a commercial EPC needs the assessment boundary and available building information.

Before you enquire

Display Energy Certificates: useful answers

Is a DEC the same as a commercial EPC?

No. A DEC describes operational energy performance for a qualifying building; a commercial EPC is a standardised asset rating.

Can shared meters make the assessment more complex?

Yes. Identify shared supplies and any available allocation information so the evidence requirements can be reviewed.

Do I need a new advisory report as well?

Send the existing documents. Their applicability and validity need checking before deciding the scope.
