Air-conditioning energy assessment

Understand what the inspection covers.

Start with the system

TM44 concerns the energy performance of air conditioning. The assessment considers efficiency, sizing, controls and operation in relation to the areas served. It is an inspection and report, rather than an instruction to carry out repairs.

Check whether it applies

In England, the requirement applies to systems with an effective rated cooling output of more than 12kW. Individual units under common technical control can need to be considered together. A small-looking installation is not enough evidence to decide the position.

Keep the work distinct

A commercial EPC, refrigerant leak check and routine maintenance serve different purposes. Tell us which records or services you need so the proposed scope does not imply that one assessment replaces another.

Your questions

TM44 enquiries in Lichfield

What should I explain about commercial premises with limited records?

Photographs and earlier maintenance schedules can support the scoping discussion; provide them without arranging unsafe access to obtain them. The quotation should identify the premises and equipment included, with responsibility and permissions confirmed before a visit.

Can you quote if I do not know the cooling capacity?

Send the available asset information, model numbers and accessible photographs. Explain what you do not know. The equipment details need review before the inspection requirement and scope can be confirmed.

How often do qualifying systems need inspection?

Government guidance specifies inspections at intervals no more than five years apart for systems in scope. Provide the earlier report so its date, coverage and subsequent installation changes can be reviewed.

Does requesting a quotation confirm an appointment?

No. The property details, agreed scope, price and access arrangements need confirmation. A statutory report must be prepared by an appropriately accredited air-conditioning energy assessor; the quotation should confirm the delivery arrangements.

Read the government air-conditioning inspection guidance for the statutory requirements. Use the guidance alongside the current equipment records when reviewing your inspection requirement.
